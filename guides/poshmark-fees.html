Poshmark fees explained

Poshmark (US) takes one fee from each sale, and the buyer pays shipping to Poshmark. Rates checked October 2026; your sale summary in the app is the final word.

  • Under $15: a flat $2.95.
  • $15 and up: 20% of the sale price.
SaleFeeItem costYou keep
$12.00 top$2.95$4.00$5.05
$30.00 jacket$6.00$8.00$16.00

At $14.99 the fee is $2.95; at $15.00 it's $3.00, so prices just under $15 lose little. On the $30 jacket, the price for a 30% margin is $16.00: $8.00 ÷ (1 − 0.20 − 0.30).
