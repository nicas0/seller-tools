Wedding budget breakdown

Decide the total first, then split it. Here is a common starting split worked through for a $20,000 wedding. Move the percentages to fit what matters most to you.

Keep it on track

  • Keep a buffer. Something always costs more than the quote.
  • Track deposits and balances. Know what's paid, what's left and when each payment is due.
  • Guest count drives cost. Catering is usually priced per head, so the guest list is a budget decision.

Not financial advice; prices vary widely by place and season.
