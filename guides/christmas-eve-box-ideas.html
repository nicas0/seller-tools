Christmas Eve box ideas

A Christmas Eve box is a small box opened on the evening of December 24: something cosy to wear, something to do, a treat, and a little magic for the night ahead. It doesn't have to cost much. Here's what goes in, by age and by budget.

The four things most boxes have

  • Something to wear tonight: new pyjamas, cosy socks or a Christmas T-shirt.
  • Something to do together: a Christmas film, a short book, a puzzle, or cookies to decorate for Santa.
  • A treat: hot chocolate with marshmallows, popcorn for the film, or a candy cane.
  • A bit of magic: a letter from Santa, a Nice List certificate, reindeer food to sprinkle outside, or a "from the North Pole" tag on the box.

Ideas by age

AgeIdeas that work
Toddlers (1-3)Pyjamas, a board book about Christmas, a soft toy, bath bubbles for a calm evening
Little kids (4-7)Pyjamas, a letter from Santa, reindeer food, a Christmas colouring page, cookie decorating
Big kids (8-12)A chapter book, a card or board game, a Nice List certificate, slime or a craft kit
TeensFluffy socks, a face mask, their favourite snack, a film night with the family
Grown-upsGood hot chocolate, a candle, a crossword book, matching family pyjamas

On a budget

  • About $10: a mug, hot chocolate sachets, a candy cane and a printed letter from Santa.
  • About $25: add pyjamas from a value store and a short book from the library sale.
  • The box itself: a shoebox wrapped in brown paper with a printed "Christmas Eve" tag looks the part.

Make it feel like it came from the North Pole

Children notice the details. A letter that uses their name, mentions something good they did this year and knows what they're hoping for is the part they keep. A Nice List certificate with their name on it goes on the fridge for weeks. Gift tags "From Santa" in handwriting that isn't yours help too.

When to put it together

Buy the pyjamas and book in early December, before the shops get busy. Print any letters and certificates the week before, on cardstock if you have it, and fill the box on the 23rd so the 24th is calm.
