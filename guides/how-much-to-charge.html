How much to charge: the price for the margin you want

Most pricing advice starts from your costs. The useful question is: what price leaves me, say, 30% of what the buyer pays, after the platform's fees, the product and the shipping?

The formula

Price = (cost to make + shipping label + packaging + fixed fees) ÷ (1 − percentage fees − target margin) − shipping you charge

Fixed fees are the per-order and per-item amounts (on Etsy US: $0.25 processing plus the $0.20 listing fee). Percentage fees are the ones taken as a share of what the buyer pays (on Etsy US: 6.5% transaction + 3% processing = 9.5%).

Example: Etsy, 30% margin

A candle costs $6.50 to make and $4.50 to ship, with free shipping for the buyer:

($6.50 + $4.50 + $0 + $0.45) ÷ (1 − 0.095 − 0.30) = $11.45 ÷ 0.605 = $18.93.

Check: at $18.93, Etsy's fees come to $2.25 and costs to $11.00, leaving $5.68, which is 30.0% of $18.93.

Example: Shopify, 30% margin

Same candle on Shopify Payments (Basic plan, US online cards: 2.9% + $0.30):

($6.50 + $4.50 + $0.30) ÷ (1 − 0.029 − 0.30) = $11.30 ÷ 0.671 = $16.84.

Or let the free Etsy or Shopify fee calculator work it out.

Good to know

  • Some margins can't be reached once fees are in: on Etsy US, no price gives 90.5% or more, because the formula's bottom part (1 − 0.095 − margin) reaches zero.
  • Fees are rounded per order, so real results can be a cent or two off.
  • Rates change and differ by country and plan; check your statement.
