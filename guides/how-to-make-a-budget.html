How to make a budget that works

A budget is a plan for your money before the month starts, and a check afterwards. Four steps.

1. Write down your income

Use what actually lands in your account after tax. Example: two paychecks of $1,600 = $3,200.

2. Plan each category

Rent $1,200, groceries $400, transport $150, utilities and phone $180, eating out $120, fun $100, other $100: $2,250 planned, leaving $950 to save.

3. Write down what you spend

Every purchase, in its category. It takes a minute a day and shows where the money really goes.

4. Compare at the end of the month
