Shopify fees per order, explained

Unlike Etsy, Shopify charges no listing or per-sale transaction fee when you use Shopify Payments. What you pay per order is card processing, plus your monthly plan.

The three parts

  • Payment processing (Shopify Payments): on the Basic plan, US online cards are 2.9% + $0.30 per order. Higher plans pay a lower rate; international cards and currency conversion cost more.
  • Third-party transaction fee: only if you use another payment provider instead of Shopify Payments (2% on Basic), on top of that provider's own fees.
  • Your plan: a monthly fee, the same however many orders you have. Spread it over your orders to see its real cost per sale.

Example: an $18.00 order

Rates differ by plan and country and change over time: check Settings > Payments in your Shopify admin and your bills. Shopify's own rate list and plan prices are on its pricing page.
