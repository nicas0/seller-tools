Mercari fees explained

Mercari (US) takes a 10% selling fee on the item price plus any shipping the buyer pays. Rates checked October 2026; Mercari has changed its fees several times, so your sale summary is the final word.

SaleFeeItem + labelYou keep
$25.00 item, free shipping$2.50$13.00$9.50
Same, with 2.9% + $0.30 processing$3.53$13.00$8.47

Some sales have shown a payment processing fee (2.9% + $0.30) as well; if yours do, the free calculator has boxes for it. For a 30% margin on the first example, charge about $21.67: $13.00 ÷ (1 − 0.10 − 0.30).
