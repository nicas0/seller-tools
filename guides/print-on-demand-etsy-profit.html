Print on demand on Etsy: what a $24 T-shirt really leaves you

With print on demand your cost isn't materials: it's what the print provider (Printful, Printify or another) charges you for the item and for shipping it to your buyer. Here is one sale, with Etsy US fees.

LineAmount
Buyer pays (T-shirt, free shipping)$24.00
Provider base cost-$12.95
Provider shipping to your buyer-$4.75
Etsy transaction fee (6.5%)-$1.56
Etsy payment processing (3% + $0.25)-$0.97
Etsy listing renewal-$0.20
You keep$3.57

That's 14.9% of the sale. One Offsite Ads sale (15%) would take another $3.60 and leave you about nothing.

The price for the margin you want

Add the provider's charges and Etsy's fixed fees, then divide by what's left after Etsy's percentage fees and your margin: ($12.95 + $4.75 + $0.25 + $0.20) ÷ (1 − 0.065 − 0.03 − 0.30) = $18.15 ÷ 0.605 = $30.00 for a 30% margin.

If your provider charges you sales tax, or prices change with the shirt size or colour, add those to the cost. Provider prices change, so copy them from your own order or product page.
