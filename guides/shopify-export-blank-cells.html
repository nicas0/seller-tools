Fill the blank cells in a Shopify orders export

Shopify puts each item of an order on its own row, but fills the email, date, status and shipping details only on the order's first row. That's by design, and it makes sorting and counting awkward.

In Excel

  1. Copy the sheet first, so you can go back.
  2. Select the columns to fill (for example Email, Created at, Shipping City), then Home > Find & Select > Go To Special > Blanks.
  3. Type =, click the cell just above, and press Ctrl+Enter.
  4. Copy the columns and Paste Values, so the formulas become fixed values.

Don't fill the Total column (or Subtotal, Shipping, Taxes). Those are amounts for the whole order; copying them onto every item row makes your totals add up several times over.
