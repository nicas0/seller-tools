Etsy vs Shopify fees: when does Shopify cost less?

The same $18.00 item with free shipping, made for $6.50 and shipped with a $4.50 label. US rates, checked October 2026.

EtsyShopify (Basic)
Fees on the sale$2.16$0.82
You keep per sale$4.84$6.18
Monthly plannone$39.00

Shopify keeps $1.34 more per sale, so the $39 plan pays for itself after 30 sales a month ($39.00 ÷ $1.34 = 29.1). Below that, Etsy costs less in fees.

Fees aren't the whole story: Etsy brings buyers to you, while on Shopify you find every buyer yourself, often with ads that cost more than the fees saved. Many sellers keep Etsy and add Shopify later.
