Comparing events

Keep the same few numbers for every market: sales, cost of goods, booth fee, card fees, other costs and hours. A busy fair with a $200 booth fee can earn less per hour than a quiet one at $40. After a few events, the ones worth going back to are obvious.

The card fee here is Square's US in-person rate (2.6% + 15 cents); check your own reader's rate. Count the cost of what sold, not everything you brought: unsold stock goes home with you.
