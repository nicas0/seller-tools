Get your small shop ready for the holidays

For most small shops, November and December are the biggest months of the year. A little planning in October means fewer late nights and missed orders.

1. Write down the dates

  • Small Business Saturday, Black Friday and Cyber Monday for this year.
  • Your carriers' holiday shipping cut-offs (USPS, UPS and FedEx publish them each autumn; they change every year).
  • Your own last day to order for Christmas delivery: the carrier cut-off minus your processing time.

2. Make and stock early

Look at last year's best sellers (or this year's, if you're new) and make enough before November. Order materials now: suppliers get slow too.

3. Plan two or three promotions, not ten

  • Free shipping over an amount raises the average order.
  • Gift sets and bundles sell well as presents.
  • An early offer to past buyers rewards the people most likely to buy again.

Check each one still leaves you money: a 20% discount on an $18 item that leaves $4.84 is $3.60 off, leaving $1.58 after the lower fees. The free Etsy fee calculator works it out.

4. Set up the shop

  • Holiday processing times and your last order date in the shop announcement.
  • Gift wrap and gift message options, if you offer them.
  • Vacation dates, if you take a break.

5. Afterwards, write it down

Orders, sales, costs, profit, the best seller and the best promotion. Next year starts with those numbers.
