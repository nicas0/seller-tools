Amazon seller fees explained (orders you ship yourself)

From Amazon's selling pricing page, checked October 2026. Fulfilment by Amazon (FBA) adds its own fees, which aren't covered here; Seller Central is the final word.

  • Referral fee: 15% in most categories, of the item price plus shipping, at least $0.30 per item. Some categories differ (clothing is tiered, for example).
  • Closing fee: $1.80 per item on books, music, video and software, which have no $0.30 minimum.
  • Selling plan: Individual costs $0.99 per item sold; Professional is $39.99 a month.

The paperback loses money: the $1.80 closing fee is a big share of a $10 sale. The Individual plan's $0.99 adds up: past about 41 sales a month, Professional's $39.99 costs less.
