Etsy fees explained

What Etsy takes from a sale in the US, in the order it happens. Rates change and differ by country: your Etsy payment account statement and Etsy's fee policy are the final word.

On every sale

  • Listing fee: $0.20. Paid to list an item for four months or until it sells; each time one sells, the listing renews for another $0.20.
  • Transaction fee: 6.5% of what the buyer pays for the item, shipping and gift wrap.
  • Payment processing: 3% + $0.25 per order (US rate).

Only sometimes

  • Offsite Ads: 15% of an order that came from one of Etsy's ads on other sites, or 12% for shops that made $10,000 or more in the past year; capped at $100 per order. Shops above $10,000 can't opt out.
  • Currency conversion: 2.5%, only if your listings are in a different currency from your bank account.
  • Regulatory operating fee: a small percentage in some countries only (not the US).
  • Etsy Ads: optional, a daily budget you set.
