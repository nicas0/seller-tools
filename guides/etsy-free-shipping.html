Etsy free shipping vs charging for shipping

Etsy's 6.5% transaction fee and 3% + $0.25 processing fee are charged on the item price and the shipping you charge. So moving shipping into the price doesn't change the fees.

$18 + $4.50 shipping$22.50, free shipping
Buyer pays$22.50$22.50
Transaction fee (6.5%)-$1.46-$1.46
Processing (3% + $0.25)-$0.93-$0.93
Listing fee-$0.20-$0.20
Cost to make, shipping label-$11.00-$11.00
You keep$8.91$8.91

Same buyer total, same fees, same profit. What changes is how the listing looks: buyers compare the price they see first, and Etsy says it gives US listings with free shipping (or free shipping on orders of $35 and up) a boost in search. Check Etsy's current shipping help pages for the details.

If your label costs more than you charged, the difference comes out of your profit either way: price from your real label cost.
