eBay fees explained

For most categories in the US, eBay takes a final value fee of 13.6% of the whole sale, shipping and sales tax included, plus a per-order fee of $0.30 (orders of $10 or less) or $0.40. Rates from eBay's selling fees page, checked October 2026; some categories differ.
