How to export your orders from Shopify

  1. In your Shopify admin, go to Orders. Filter or sort first if you only want some orders.
  2. Click Export.
  3. Choose which orders: the current page (up to 50) downloads straight away; for more, choose Orders by date and set the dates, and Shopify emails the file to you and the store owner.
  4. Click Export orders for all order information (one row per item), or Export transaction histories for payments only.

Big exports can take a while: Shopify says under an hour for fewer than 100,000 items. If one fails, you get an email. (Steps from Shopify's help page, checked October 2026.)

Before you work with it

  • Each item of an order is its own row, and order details (email, date, total) are only on the first row. See filling the blank cells and why totals look wrong.
  • Opening it in Excel can garble accented letters and drop leading zeros: how to avoid that.
