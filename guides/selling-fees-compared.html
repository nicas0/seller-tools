Selling fees compared: the same $30 sale on six apps

One item sold for $30.00, which cost you $8.00. The buyer pays shipping on top. US rates checked in October 2026; every app changes its fees from time to time, so your sale summary is the final word.

What the table leaves out

  • Shipping: Mercari, Depop, eBay and Etsy also take their percentage of the shipping the buyer pays (and eBay and Depop of the sales tax). Poshmark's buyers pay shipping to Poshmark, so it isn't counted.
  • Shopify charges a monthly plan, and you bring your own buyers; the others find buyers for you.
  • Ads: Etsy's Offsite Ads (12-15%), eBay's promoted listings and Depop's boosted listings add a fee on the sales they bring in.
  • Under $15 on Poshmark the fee is a flat $2.95, not 20%.

The cheapest fee isn't always the most money: an app with more buyers for your kind of item can sell it faster or for more. Put your own numbers into each calculator above to compare your items.
