Depop fees explained

In the US, Depop charges no selling fee on most sales. You pay payment processing of 3.3% + $0.45 on the whole amount the buyer pays, shipping and sales tax included. Rates checked October 2026.

Shipping the buyer pays$5.00

Processing: 3.3% of $32.40 + $0.45-$1.52
Item cost and shipping label-$13.00
You keep (the tax goes to the state)$15.48

Boosted listings add an extra fee on the sales they bring in; it isn't included here.
