Etsy's Orders CSV vs Order Items CSV

Etsy lets you download your sales as two different files. Picking the right one saves a lot of spreadsheet work.

Where to find them

Shop Manager > Settings > Options > Download Data. Under Orders, choose the type (Orders or Order Items), the month and the year, then download the CSV.

Orders: one row per order

Each row is a whole order: sale date, order ID, buyer, number of items, order value, discounts, shipping, sales tax and order total. Use it for monthly totals and for matching payments.

Order Items: one row per item sold

Each row is one item in an order: item name, quantity, price, item total, transaction ID, listing ID and SKU, with the order ID repeated on each of an order's rows. Use it for which products sell and what each one earns. A 3-item order appears 3 times here; that's not a duplicate.

Profit per product

The free Profit Check reads the Order Items file, takes off discounts, estimates Etsy's fees from rates you can change, and shows profit per product and per month once you add your costs.
