Debt snowball vs avalanche

Both methods pay every minimum and put everything extra on one debt at a time. They only differ in which debt comes first.

  • Snowball: smallest balance first. Quick wins keep you going.
  • Avalanche: highest interest rate first. Least interest in total.

Avalanche saves $35.93 here; snowball clears the first debt seven months sooner. With big gaps between rates the avalanche saving grows; when balances are small, the snowball's early wins can matter more.

What matters more than the method

Paying extra. With minimums only (the same debts, no extra), it takes 61 months and $4,486.69 in interest. The $200 a month saves 21 months and $2,101.67, whichever method you choose.
