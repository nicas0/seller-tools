Why your Shopify export totals look wrong

Shopify's orders export has one row per item, but order amounts (Total, Subtotal, Shipping, Taxes, Discount Amount) appear only on each order's first row. That's why some quick sums come out wrong.

What to add up

  • Revenue: sum the Total column as exported. The blanks on item rows are correct: each order's total is counted once.
  • Number of orders: count the different order numbers (the Name column, like #1001), not the rows. A 3-item order has 3 rows. In Excel: =SUMPRODUCT(1/COUNTIF(A2:A500,A2:A500)) on the Name column (no blank cells in that range).
  • Items sold: sum Lineitem quantity.

What makes totals wrong

  • Filling the blank cells of the Total column with the order's total (copying it down) makes a 3-item order count three times. Fill down only details like email, date and city.
  • Adding Lineitem price without multiplying by Lineitem quantity undercounts items bought more than once.
  • Lineitem price x quantity is before the order's discounts, shipping and tax, so it won't match Total.
