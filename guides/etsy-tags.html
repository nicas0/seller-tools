How to write Etsy tags

Tags are the phrases Etsy uses, along with your title, to match your listing to what buyers search for. You get 13, of up to 20 characters each.

The rules that matter

  • Use all 13. An empty tag is a search you can't show up in.
  • Use phrases, not single words. "soy candle gift" matches the way people search better than "candle".
  • Write what buyers would type: what it is, what it's for, who it's for, the style, the occasion.
  • Don't repeat a tag, and don't spend tags on your shop's category, which Etsy already knows.
  • Match your title. The important phrases should be in both.
  • 20 characters each, spaces included; long phrases need trimming.

Example: a lavender soy candle

Thirteen different phrases, each under 20 characters, covering the scent, the material, who it's for and when people buy it.
