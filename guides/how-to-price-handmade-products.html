How to price handmade products

Price from what an item really costs you, including your time, then check what's left after fees. Worked through for one 8 oz soy candle.

1. Materials per item, from what you paid for each pack

Fragrance oil$24 for 16 oz$1.50/oz0.6 oz$0.90
Wick$8 for 100$0.081$0.08
Jar$18 for 12$1.501$1.50
Label$10 for 100$0.101$0.10
Materials$4.33

2. Add packaging, your time and overhead

  • Packaging: $0.50
  • Your time: 6 minutes at $20 an hour = $2.00. Leave this out and you're working for free.
  • Overhead (tools, electricity, spoiled batches): 10% of $6.83 = $0.68

Cost to make: $4.33 + $0.50 + $2.00 + $0.68 = $7.51.

3. Wholesale and retail

A common rule for handmade goods: wholesale = 2 × cost = $15.02, and retail = 2 × wholesale = $30.04. Shops buy at wholesale and sell at retail, so pricing this way lets you sell to them later without losing money. Your market may support more or less: these are starting points, not rules.

4. Check what's left after fees

Selling the candle on Etsy for $28: fees are $1.82 (6.5%) + $0.84 + $0.25 (processing) + $0.20 (listing) = $3.11. You keep $28.00 − $3.11 − $7.51 = $17.38 on top of the $2.00 you paid yourself.
