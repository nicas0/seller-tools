Etsy title and tag checker

Paste a listing's title and tags to check them against Etsy's limits. Everything is checked in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

Tags used: -

Etsy's limits: titles up to 140 characters, up to 13 tags, each up to 20 characters. Using all 13 and not repeating a tag gives your listing the most ways to be found. Words in your title that no tag uses are listed as ideas, not mistakes. Etsy can change its limits; its listing editor is the final word.
