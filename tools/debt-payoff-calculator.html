How it works: your monthly budget stays the same (every minimum plus the extra). Each month every debt gets interest (balance × APR ÷ 12) and its minimum; what's left goes to the debts in order (snowball: smallest balance first; avalanche: highest rate first), so money freed when a debt is paid off moves straight on to the next. Lenders may work out interest daily, so real statements can differ a little. Not financial advice.
