    The USDA's times

    Oven at 325 °F. Times are approximate: the turkey is done when a food thermometer reads 165 °F in the thickest part of the thigh, the wing and the thickest part of the breast. Let it rest 20 minutes before carving.

    WeightFridge thawingRoasting, unstuffedRoasting, stuffed
    8 to 12 pounds1 to 3 days2¾ to 3 hours3 to 3½ hours
    12 to 14 pounds3 to 4 days3 to 3¾ hours3½ to 4 hours
    14 to 16 pounds3 to 4 days3¾ to 4¼ hours4 to 4¼ hours
    16 to 18 pounds4 to 5 days3¾ to 4¼ hours4 to 4¼ hours
    18 to 20 pounds4 to 5 days4¼ to 4½ hours4¼ to 4¾ hours
    20 to 24 pounds5 to 6 days4½ to 5 hours4¾ to 5¼ hours

    Thawing in cold water takes about 30 minutes per pound: keep the turkey wrapped, change the water every 30 minutes, and cook it straight away. A turkey thawed in the fridge keeps there for 1 to 2 more days, so the calculator plans with the longer thawing time; on a boundary weight (12, 16, 20 pounds) it uses the longer row.

    Source: USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service, "Let's Talk Turkey: A Consumer Guide to Safely Roasting a Turkey". This calculator is a planning aid, not a substitute for a food thermometer. Outside 8 to 24 pounds, see the USDA's guide.
