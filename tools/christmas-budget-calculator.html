Keeping to it

  • Set the total first, then split it: a budget built from a gift list tends to grow.
  • Write each gift down as you buy it, with what it cost, so the gifts line shows what's left.
  • Cards and postage add up: overseas cards and parcels cost more, and the post gets busy in December.

The starting split is just a place to begin, not advice: every household's Christmas is different.
