Two small tools for Etsy, Shopify and Amazon sellers. Both run in your browser with no internet access, so your orders never leave your computer. Try them free on files up to 50 rows.

Finds the real duplicate orders (an order with 3 items appearing 3 times isn't one), fills Shopify's blank order details on item rows, renames messy columns and makes a one-row-per-order summary. Every row is kept, with a plain-English log of what changed.
